Battery paper trading, live track record

Every morning before the day-ahead gate closure a forecast of tomorrow's prices and an optimal 1 MW / 2 MWh battery plan; the next day it is settled on real prices and appended to a ledger that is never recomputed.

Backtest since June 2024: forecast + MILP earns 83 % of perfect foresight (324 k PLN/MW/yr), but a plain 7-day price average does as well; live record from 28 Oct 2026.

Solar PV

PV outages: detection, cause and availability

Seven ways to find production losses at string, inverter and meter level (threshold, expected PR model, peer median, Hampel, CUSUM, isolation forest, gradient boosting) plus a hybrid, cause classification, lost energy and IEC 61724-1 style availability on a simulated plant with ground truth, real PVDAQ data and an open shading dataset.

Requiring events of at least 1 h lifts the hybrid detector's precision from 0.73 to 0.97 and cuts alarms about 20-fold; thresholds tuned on simulation give 257 alarms per inverter-year on real roofs, a short site set-up brings it to 6 with all real outages still found.

  • IEC 61724-1

A Bayesian digital twin of a wind farm

A probabilistic model of every Kelmarsh turbine updated day by day from SCADA: posteriors with credible intervals for efficiency, anemometer error, yaw offset, derating and bearing cooling, the probability of each fault type, lost energy by cause and inspections ranked by value of information.

On 624 injected faults it names the right cause in 95 % of detections against 72 % for CUSUM and NBM charts, with 0.50 false alarms per turbine-year against 2.08.

Wind turbine downtime, causes and availability

Stops, deratings and curtailment found in 10-minute SCADA without status codes (rules, power-curve residual, pitch / rotor-speed signature, HMM, gradient boosting), classified into IEC 61400-26 categories, priced in lost energy and turned into time- and energy-based availability on three open farms, with a CLI for any farm's long-format SCADA.

The pitch / rotor-speed signature finds stops with F1 0.80-0.91 without training, while the usual "power ≤ 0" rule raises 4,833 alarms for 1,412 logged stops; booking grid and curtailment stops as turbine faults cuts warranty availability from 97.9 % to 93.5 %.

  • outage detection
  • gradient boosting

Did the upgrade work? Causal O&M verification

Five causal estimators (test-control bins, gradient boosting with conformal intervals, synthetic control, BSTS, difference-in-differences) validated by injecting known uplifts into real Kelmarsh wind and PVDAQ PV data, with a test-plan calculator, wake spillover and real logged events.

A +3 % uplift needs about 9 weeks to prove with five control turbines, +1 % stays unproven after a year; on real events before/after reports -3.2 % to +3.0 % where the turbines came back within ±1 %.

Can a fly brain forecast wind and solar?

The FlyWire connectome of the fruit fly brain used as an echo state network reservoir to forecast Polish wind and PV generation, against random and rewired reservoirs, ridge, gradient boosting and the PSE day-ahead plan.

No: tuned separately, the fly brain matches a random reservoir within 2 %; its compressed eigenvalue spectrum makes it robust to bad settings, not a better forecaster.

pse-opendata: Python client for PSE data

An open-source library for the public data of the Polish TSO: 16 reports plus the legacy CSV export since 2009 as tidy pandas frames with explicit interval times in UTC and local time, monthly Parquet cache aware of PSE's late corrections, and a CLI.

103 offline tests on recorded responses plus live checks against PSE's API description; documents pitfalls such as DST labels, the 100,000-row cap and pagination that drops rows.

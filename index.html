Battery arbitrage on Polish prices

Mixed-integer dispatch of a battery on 15-minute RCE prices, solved for every delivery day, with sensitivity to duration, efficiency and cycle limits.

859-day backtest: a 2-hour battery earned 308 k PLN per MW in 2025, and negative prices reached 354 hours that year.

Storage & markets

How real batteries trade

One year of 5-minute AEMO data for the 40 batteries of 20 MW or more in Australia's NEM, compared day by day with perfect-foresight arbitrage.

The six largest batteries earn 11-51 % of their perfect-foresight optimum; the gap is split into capacity offered, volume and timing.

Solar PVOwn method

PV shading from AC power alone

Sun-path residual fingerprinting finds, draws and costs obstructions from 15-minute AC power, without an irradiance sensor, a site visit or a drone.

A building's silhouette recovered with mask IoU 0.93 and 0.8 deg RMSE on simulated systems, then run on two years of a real PVDAQ roof.

Solar PV

Single-axis trackers in 3D

Row-to-row shading projected onto module cells with a bypass-diode string model: true tracking vs backtracking vs slope-aware backtracking near Poznań.

Backtracking gives +4.3 % DC yield over true tracking on flat ground (1255 vs 1204 kWh/kWp).

Solar PV

PV fault detection: autoencoder vs baselines

A PyTorch autoencoder against a PR threshold, an ASTM E2848 regression and a pvlib model, scored per fault type on 20 injected faults and on real PVDAQ data.

F1 0.77 against 0.37-0.48 for the baselines; on real data it caught an inverter outage reported 10 days late by the inverter itself.

  • PyTorch
  • anomaly detection
  • NREL PVDAQ

WindOwn method

WARP: wake-aware turbine verification

Expected power from the unwaked neighbours carried through a calibrated wake model, with CUSUM charts for power loss, derating, anemometer bias and yaw misalignment.

Validated on 288 injected faults: a 4 deg yaw misalignment is found in 100 % of cases (median 6 days), standard references find 6-10 %.

  • PyWake
  • CUSUM
  • SCADA

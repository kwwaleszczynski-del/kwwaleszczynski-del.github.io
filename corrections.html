Corrections

Every project here is re-read from time to time by an independent reviewer who gets the code and the data, not the conclusions. When a headline number or a conclusion does not hold, it is corrected in the repository and listed below: what was claimed, what is claimed now and why. Results that only reproduced are not listed.

biogas-digester-twin16 Nov 2026

Was
CUSUM on the methane yield detects 100 % of process upsets, EWMA and the expert rules nearly as many; PCA on online signals warns before the yield loss in 75 % of upsets.
Now
Within 14 days and against chance: CUSUM 29 % (chance 14 %), EWMA 89 % (29 %), expert rules 82 % (29 %), PCA 54 % (2 %), gradient boosting 92 % (6 %), EKF twin 89 % (15 %). Early warning before a material loss exists for ammonia inhibition only; the other upsets are mild in this plant or lose yield from the first day.
Why
Any alarm start within 60 days of the cause counted as a detection with no chance level, while EWMA and the rules are in alarm on about 40 % of normal days and the CUSUM on 76 %. Test runs had shared one feed history, and harmless recipe changes had not overlapped the upset sizes.

wind-wake-aware-verification16 Nov 2026

Was
The method finds a derating to 95 % of rated power in 94 % of cases where the neighbour index finds 4 %, and a 3 % anemometer bias in 83 %; without the wake model detection drops.
Now
With a full-load chart for every method and thresholds at one false-alarm rate, the turbine's own power curve is the better full-load detector (derating 100 % in 6 days against 96 % in 31 days). The method's distinct result is the anemometer bias in either direction (90 % against a chance of 12 %). The wake model's gain is confined to partial load.
Why
The baselines had no full-load chart, so they could not see a derating by construction, and thresholds set at the calibration-year maximum gave the methods different false-alarm rates; the gap without the wake model was a threshold artefact.

pv-anomaly-autoencoder16 Nov 2026

Was
The autoencoder detects 17 of 20 injected fault events.
Now
15 of the 17 events that change the output at all, when at least half of an event's affected daylight steps must raise an alarm (1.6 by chance); the baselines detect 5-7. Over five other seeds: 37 of 81 against 4.7 by chance. Average precision 0.91 against 0.60-0.77.
Why
One alarmed step anywhere in an event counted as a detection, which alarms shifted in time by whole days reach for 6.5-9.7 events; three injected events never affect the output, and the thresholds had different false-positive rates on the test period.

wind-bayes-twin16 Nov 2026

Was
The twin's first alarm names the right cause in 95 % of detections against 72 % for the charts; nothing was tuned on the test years.
Now
Of all 624 faults the twin names 84 % and the charts 70 %. The advantage is confined to telling an efficiency loss from a derating: without the efficiency-loss scenarios the two are level (91 % against 86 %, and 92 % against 91 % at matched false-alarm rates). Detection and the false-alarm rate (0.50 against 2.08 per turbine-year) stand. All validation numbers are upper bounds.
Why
The charts' full-load chart was mapped to derating, so one mapping produced most of the gap. Several design choices were revised after looking at 2019-2020 results, and the injected faults are near-exact shifts of the statistics the twin models.

wind-thermal-nbm16 Nov 2026

Was
The RC parameters give a health trend the black box does not: the rear generator bearing's rise at rated power grows from 16.5 to 25.4 K on one turbine. On the CARE benchmark the temperature models are precise but miss faults no thermometer can see.
Now
The trend was a fit artefact: with the time constant, intercept and cooling mixture held at the first year's fit, the yearly rise follows the measured rise at rated power (median gap 0.1-0.6 K). On CARE the models detect nothing beyond chance (as many fault datasets flagged as with circularly shifted alarms). Detection of a +2 °C drift now has intervals: 76 % [71-81] for the RC model to 91 % [87-94] for gradient boosting with the fleet median.
Why
Refitting every parameter each year let the temperature rise trade off against the time constant (0.9-1.9 h, then 3.2 h in 2021). The CARE detections had not been compared with a placebo, and the alarm design was settled with the test years in view.

bess-ops-warranty16 Nov 2026

Was
Median round-trip efficiency 0.82 across 55 NEM batteries; a window whose one-way efficiency could not be determined used a constant and passed.
Now
0.825 for the 52 batteries with an RTE; three have no RTE for the year and are listed as no data. A window that cannot be assessed is reported with its reason and counted next to the damages, not as a window without damages. One validation series moved from 0.736 to 0.723.
Why
The yearly RTE had counted the energy of months whose change of stored energy was unknown and taken that change as zero, and an undetermined efficiency had been replaced by 0.95.

pv-outage-classifier16 Nov 2026

Was
The hybrid detector has the highest recall (0.86) and the expected model the best F1 (0.80); all real outages on the PVDAQ roofs are found; fleet availability 98.3-99.0 %.
Now
Much of the recall is coincidence: the hybrid's alarms shifted in time by whole days still find 40 % of the events. By excess recall Hampel, the isolation forest, the expected model and the hybrid are level (0.46-0.52), and the order changes under a stricter rule. Soiling is barely detected (8 % of episodes half covered). Availability in 2016 is 98.3 %, 95.8 % or 91.4 % depending on how gaps in the inverter data are counted.
Why
One alarm anywhere inside an event that lasts weeks counted as a detection, and the real outages are defined by the same zero-output rule the threshold detector uses. Outages that continued as data gaps were taken out of the availability denominator. The site set-up is now tuned on 2016 and reported on 2017-2018.

pv-thermal-check16 Nov 2026

Was
The cells run 19 K above the back-of-module sensor, the signature of a loose sensor; injected sensor faults are found on all 40 start dates; corrected PR leaves a winter dip explained by shading and an autumn decline by soiling.
Now
The gap is 15-20 K depending on the calibration and rests on one 12-day open-circuit window; a loose sensor is one explanation among others. With no fault injected, 20 % of the start dates already count as detected, so detection is reported on the 32 clean start dates. The residual PR pattern depends on the irradiance scaling and is not attributed to a cause. Annual temperature loss 10.9 % (was 10.5-10.8 %), Poland 3.5-4.5 % (was 3.2-4.2 %).
Why
The clean record has an alarm of its own inside the detection horizon of 8 start dates; the seasonal residual changes sign between one irradiance scale factor and month-specific factors; and the cell model had used the STC efficiency instead of the measured operating efficiency.

wind-om-reliability15 Nov 2026

Was
Crow-AMSAA separates an improving fleet (Kelmarsh, beta 0.82) from a deteriorating one (Penmanshiel, beta 1.12); Hill of Towie improves to 4-5 failures per turbine-year.
Now
Kelmarsh still improves (beta 0.81, 0.76-0.91). Penmanshiel shows no clear trend (0.95, 0.89-1.01). The Hill of Towie trend is not assessed.
Why
The Penmanshiel figure came from repeating alarms on three turbines and an interval that assumed independent events; the trend is now fitted on repeat chains with a turbine bootstrap. The Hill of Towie code dictionary was built from one year, so other years were not comparable. Failure counts are now published with their sensitivity to the counting rule (Kelmarsh 3.6-8.2, Penmanshiel 10.8-19.3 per turbine-year).

pl-bess-paper-trading15 Nov 2026

Was
A battery that trades on paper every day; scheduled runs start 30-60 minutes late and one day has no plan.
Now
Of the first 20 live days 6 have a plan and 14 do not; the model earned 11.7 % of perfect foresight over all days and 52 % on the days it traded. The live record is too short for conclusions.
Why
GitHub started the scheduled runs 6-10 hours late, after the market gate, so most mornings made no plan; the plans that exist were started by an external trigger. The ledger itself was checked and is append-only: no plan was made after prices were known.

pl-capture-price15 Nov 2026

Was
Every 10 pp of PV share costs PV about 0.20 of value factor; a PV build-out scenario of 0.58 (+20 %) and 0.49 (+50 %), backed by a hindcast.
Now
The within-month slope is -1.69 with a standard error of 1.12, not significant; the scenario gives 0.59 (0.52-0.66) and 0.51 (0.35-0.68). No simple model beats repeating last year's value (MAE 0.080 against 0.081). RCE is described as PSE's reference price, not the SDAC price.
Why
The pooled slope was mostly seasonal, and the hindcast had been fitted with months after the target. The 2025 capture rates (PV 0.64, wind 0.94) did not change.

pl-res-curtailment15 Nov 2026

Was
Announced PV curtailment before the reports API: 520 GWh, covering 91 % of the regulator's figure for 2024.
Now
517.3 GWh, equal to 90 % of the regulator's figure; the announcement volumes are stated as announced orders, 63 % of them published before the hour began.
Why
24 September 2024 was counted in both sources, and ordered power is not a subset of the energy the regulator reports. Annual totals from the reports API did not change.

pse-opendata15 Nov 2026

Was
Cross-border flow report documented as one signed row per border.
Now
Two directional rows per border (import and export components), with a helper that nets them; an optional check re-downloads a cached month that PSE republished later (version 0.2.1).
Why
Reading one row as the net flow gave +85 MW instead of -613 MW for the Czech border in one test week. A month cached before a late republication was served as final.

res-asset-kpi12 Nov 2026

Was
KPI functions accepted records with gaps and returned a number.
Now
A window with missing meter data has no round-trip efficiency instead of a low one, missing expected power gives no energy availability instead of zero loss, partial sums are not reported as full intervals, and constants standing in for missing information raise a warning (versions 0.1.1 and 0.1.2).
Why
Missing data were silently counted as zeros: one missing discharge hour moved a daily RTE from 0.64 to 0.48 with the result still marked valid.

wind-downtime-availability12 Nov 2026

Was
The category a stop is booked under moves warranty availability more than detecting the stop does: 97.9 % becomes 93.5 %.
Now
One mis-booked farm-wide stop can outweigh all detection error: 4.1 of those 4.4 pp are a single 15-day park-controller stop. Without it the scenario costs 0.3 pp, about the size of the detection error.
Why
The headline rested on one event of one farm-year. Cause classification is also reported by stop time now (rules right for 21 % and 68 %), and at one farm it makes availability worse than no classification.

bess-ops-warranty12 Nov 2026

Was
Batteries are technically available (95.8 %) but commercially much less is offered (78.1 %).
Now
For the median battery only 3.2 % of the registered power is declared and not offered; five of 38 batteries hold back a third or more. The 95.8 % is an upper bound (capability within 24 hours' notice).
Why
The gap counted every interval in which an empty battery offered less; with more than 10 % of the energy stored, offered availability is 94.1 %.

pl-forecast-bench12 Nov 2026

Was
Intraday models beat the PSE plan by 20-26 %.
Now
Intraday models beat PSE's day-ahead plan by 10-26 %, and by 10-21 % against the plan rescaled for its bias; day-ahead intervals are wider than published.
Why
Part of the plan's error is curtailment and a systematic over-forecast (wind +225 MW on average, +1,300 MW at prices at or below zero), and the bootstrap had treated days as independent.

wind-les-gpu12 Nov 2026

Was
The LES reproduces the measured farm wake loss (9.8 % against 9.2 %) and the deep-wake sectors, with the lowest error of four models.
Now
The pooled loss matches as closely as the Gaussian model; split by wind speed and turbulence, part of the match is averaging (3 pp low in the most frequent sector at 5-8 m/s, high at 8-10 m/s). The ranking claim is dropped.
Why
One speed ratio was applied to all 5-10 m/s records, and the 0.6 pp gap sits inside the uncertainty of how the measured loss is defined.

pv-budget-bridge12 Nov 2026

Was
Outage and curtailment losses come back within 0.5 % of a known truth; PR 0.63-0.67 on the real rooftops.
Now
A 3-day outage injected into real and synthetic plants comes back within 1 %, a curtailment that binds within 3-7 %; the 0.5 % on the synthetic plant is labelled a best case. PR 0.65-0.68 on intervals with complete data.
Why
The synthetic truth followed the estimator's own bookkeeping, an outage under a partial set-point was over-charged to availability, and the summary PR counted data gaps as zero production.

scada-fault-bench12 Nov 2026

Was
Best F1 0.40 on wind and 0.53 on PV; recall 0.08-0.75 on 12 real faults.
Now
Every method detects more than chance on injected faults, but much of the raw recall is coincidence (a z-score reaches 0.91 on wind with 0.68 by chance). Best excess recall 0.42 on wind and 0.37 on PV; on the 12 real faults no method is distinguishable from chance.
Why
An alarm anywhere in a one-to-three-week window counted as a detection. Detection now requires an alarm on affected rows and is reported next to a placebo.

bess-reporting-kit12 Nov 2026

Was
A 200 MW battery earned 122 % of its as-of benchmark; at fleet scale the revenue stack stays above day-ahead arbitrage.
Now
The battery earned 99 % of a benchmark that has its own limits; at 2,000 MW the stack keeps 10-21 % of the lone battery's margin and crosses day-ahead arbitrage on the lower bound.
Why
The benchmark battery was smaller than the real one, and the fleet model was not a fixed point, so it is now given as two bounds.

scada-schema12 Nov 2026

Was
Loaders wrote the CARE benchmark's power in kW and found the PVDAQ unit switch only when it fell inside the loaded period.
Now
CARE power is stored as a scaled, dimensionless signal; the PVDAQ unit is detected from the power level; La Haute Borne's clock is reconstructed and 96 ambiguous rows are dropped (version 0.1.1).
Why
CARE power is normalised by the source, and a file that starts after the switch was read 100 times too high.
